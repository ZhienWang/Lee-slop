    How the three gym days change in this phase

    Lee himself trained almost every day, often twice. Three focused gym days will get you most of the strength and conditioning. The speed, skill and flexibility he was known for come from short daily practice, so the plan includes optional 15-minute home sessions. Turn them on in My week.

    Your gym days (pick 3)
    Gym session shape · about 1–2 hours, longer in the heavy phases
    • Warm-up, about 12 min. Jump rope, then leg swings and arm circles. Never skip this. Lee badly injured his back in 1970 doing heavy good mornings without warming up.
    • Lee's six lifts, 25–50 min. His 1965 general program: clean & press, squat, pullover, bench press, good morning, barbell curl.
    • Skill block, 15–40 min. Rotates: striking (A), conditioning (B), kicks and flexibility (C).
    • Abs & forearms, 8–20 min. Lee trained both nearly every day.
    Home session · 15 min, no equipment
    • Leg raises, sit-up twists, waist twists: 1 round each
    • Shadowboxing from a fighting stance: 3 × 2 min
    • Hamstring, hip and groin stretches: 5 min
    • Grip: squeeze a tennis ball or towel, 2 × 30 s each hand

    Keep one day fully off. Recovery is when you actually get stronger.

    Your session at 24 Hour Fitness

    The same workout, matched to equipment you'll find at a typical 24 Hour Fitness club. Each exercise shows where to go, what to use, how to set it up, and a backup if that equipment is taken or your club doesn't have it.

    Usually at every club

    Dumbbells, benches, bench-press stations, squat racks, Smith machines, cable towers, strength machines, treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, back-extension benches, pull-up / knee-raise stations

    At many clubs

    Turf zone, rowers, plyo boxes, medicine balls, battle ropes, TRX straps, BOSU balls, EZ-curl bars

    Only some clubs

    Heavy bags, trap bars, speed bags. Ask the front desk or check your club's page.

    Bring in your gym bag

    Jump rope, hand wraps and bag gloves, a small towel. A wrist roller is optional (cheap online).
